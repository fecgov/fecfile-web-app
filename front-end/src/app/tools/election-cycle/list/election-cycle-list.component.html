Election cycles

About election cycles

To create and file a Form 3, you'll need to provide the dates of the election cycle. An election cycle begins the day after the previous general election for a given federal office and ends on the date of the general election for that office.

For current election cycle dates or information on special elections, visit FEC.gov.

Keep in mind that election cycles cannot overlap, so if you've already created future cycles, you may need to update those cycles as well to reflect the new dates. In the event of a runoff, the cycle end date should be updated to the day of the runoff election.
